For one more year, I will be compiling my favourite albums and EPs released this year, plus the live shows I saw. Items are listed in rough descending order of enjoyment.

[Here's the 2022 list](/data/2022.jpeg), [[Favourite music of 2023]], [[Favourite music of 2024]], [[Favourite music of 2025]].

### Live shows

- [Vulfpeck](https://vulfpeck.bandcamp.com) @ O2 Arena, London
  - Bucket list concert of my life, and it was as good as expected. Played most of the classics and the energy was great, sad I didn't really get to dance with everyone sitting down.
- [Snarky Puppy](https://snarkypuppy.bandcamp.com) @ Noches del Botánico
  - Michael League conducting a smaller band that sounded huge, an incredible clavinet solo and Lingus left for the encore. One of the best concerts I've seen in a long while.
- Flo de Lotus @ Sala Meteoro
  - Electronic meets acoustic: five young musicians playing for real, polyrhythms, improvisation, tons of fx and modular. Kind of everything I love, and it turns out it was their first show ever.
- [Elijah Fox](https://elijahfox1.bandcamp.com) @ Don Giorgio
  - Played incredibly well with just a Nord and a multi-fx pedal, lots of playing with delays and crystallizers.
- Barcode @ Sala Meteoro
  - Extremely enjoyable sounds, with port/city aesthetics in the visuals.
- [corto.alto](https://cortoalto.bandcamp.com) @ Sala Riquela
  - Liam clearly has fun, looping keys, bass and trombone with sax and drums, playing more notes than fit in a measure, weird metric modulations and funny sound effects.
- CMS Trio @ Madrid
  - Much better than I expected: three older guys who have clearly played together for a long time, mostly standards, super honest music with no tricks.
- [Nate Smith](https://waterbabymusic.bandcamp.com) @ Noches del Botánico
  - Front row, great band, though most of the crowd was there for Snarky Puppy.
- [EPROM](https://eprom.bandcamp.com) b2b [G Jones](https://gjonesbass.bandcamp.com) @ Sneaky Pete's, Edinburgh
  - Went straight there after landing, cool music and some new stuff.
- [Black Country New Road](https://blackcountrynewroad.bandcamp.com) @ Sala Inn
  - Really good playing and I liked the range of instruments (accordion, clarinet, violin), but terrible-sounding venue.
- Tristan and the Air Jazz Band @ Don Giorgio
  - He has many of the right ideas, short but intense.
- [Jacob Khalil](https://jacobkhalilmusic.bandcamp.com) @ The Jazz Club + Charlie Heeb @ Mash
  - Stumbled into a Vulfpeck-like jazz show, then followed the drummer and bassist to their rock/blues gig later that night.
- [Baiuca](https://www.deezer.com/artist/7151160) @ Festas de Santa Cruz
- TremeTerra Fest @ Encoro de Vilasouto
  - Crazy place with a big, loud soundsystem, though the dub techno got repetitive.
- [Judeline](https://www.deezer.com/artist/78332032) @ Madrid
- [DJ Plead](https://djplead.bandcamp.com) @ Conde Duque
  - Sitting down in an auditorium, cool music but not that interesting.
- [MacroMassa](https://www.deezer.com/artist/1215933) 50th anniversary
  - Dissociative narrations over beats, quite interesting.
- WOS Festival @ Santiago

This is not an exhaustive list, just the ones that stood out the most to me.
