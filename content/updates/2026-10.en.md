I finally listened to my mom and I'm practicing yoga (Ashtanga, Mysore style) several days a week.
I recommend everyone try it (not helping the cult allegations...), especially if you spend >12 hours a day staring at screens like I do.
It's an individual practice even though there are more people in the room, and I was surprised by how hard I pushed myself without noticing, unlike at the gym, where I tend to minimize effort.

The influence of sport on the mind is still underrated. For all the focus/cognitive-enhancement tricks out there, the clarity you get from sweating for a while is unmatched.
